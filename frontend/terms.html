4. Food orders

You can pay for an order online (Razorpay, test mode) or with cash on delivery. Delivery is offered only inside Vadodara (pincodes 390001 to 390025). The delivery details you enter must be real enough to describe where an order would go, but because this is a demo you may use made-up details. Order totals are shown before you confirm. Order status shown in the app is updated by the admin.

  • An online order is placed only after the payment succeeds. An unpaid order is dropped after 15 minutes.
  • Cancel within 30 seconds of paying and you get a full (100%) refund.
  • After 30 seconds you can still cancel until the kitchen starts preparing, and 25% is refunded.
  • If the restaurant cancels a paid order, you get a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. In test mode no real money moves.

5. Table bookings and deposit

  • Choosing a slot holds the table for 2 minutes while you pay. If the deposit is not paid in that time, the hold is released.
  • A deposit of Rs. 100 confirms the booking and is adjusted in your bill.
  • If you cancel at least 6 hours before the slot, the deposit is refunded in full. Between 2 and 6 hours before, 50% is refunded. Less than 2 hours before, nothing is refunded.
  • If the restaurant cancels, the deposit is refunded in full.
  • If a payment arrives after a hold has expired and the table is no longer free, the payment is refunded automatically.

Because payments are in test mode, these rules show how a real system would behave, and no real money moves.
